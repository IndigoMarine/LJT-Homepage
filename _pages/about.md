I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng.

My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing** and **Machine Learning**, with specific interests in:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

### Academic Background
- **Ph.D. in Computer Science** (2024–Present), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)
- **B.Eng.** (2020–2024), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)
  - Recipient of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship
